An earring with 10 RGB LEDs powered by a 12mm coin cell. An ATTiny1616 MCU controls the LEDs in rows, so PWM control and patterns can be made.

The tab on the bottom is to get the battery holder assembled by JLCPCB, which then will be manually desoldered and soldered to the back of the earring. The tab on the bottom can then be cut off. 

The Keystone 3000 can be ordered from other sources, however shipping is egregiously expensive from Mouser, Digikey, etc., and double sided PCBA also adds cost. Implementing a cutaway tab allows single-side PCBA and takes advantage of JLC's stock so costs associated with double-sided PCBA can be circumvented and shipping costs only need to be paid for one item.
